# FormulaTrackr
## For you Formula 1 fans in the world.

### Easy information about statistics and schedule for you all Formula 1 fans. You can check current live standings for Driver & Constructor Championship or even to check standing from years before. Also you can check this season schedule and check stats about your favorite Driver and Teams.

Tech stack : MVVM | Jetpack Compose | Kotlin Coroutine + Flow | Hilt | Ktor | Chucker | Coil | Material 3 with Material You
